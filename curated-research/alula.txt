Seasonality, read this first:
AlUla is technically open year-round, but May to September brings extreme heat that makes outdoor exploration genuinely uncomfortable, and nearly everything worth doing in AlUla is outdoors. A summer trip is not impossible but it must be built around early mornings and evenings, with the middle of the day written off. AlUla's marquee events and festival programming (AlUla Moments) are concentrated in the cooler months, so a summer visitor also misses most of the cultural calendar. If a customer's dates fall in May-September, that is worth raising with them honestly rather than selling a normal itinerary.

Rental cars and getting around:
CRITICAL: there is no public transport in AlUla, and the sites are spread across a very large area (the region covers roughly 22,500 square kilometres, and Hegra alone sits about 22 km north of AlUla town centre). A car, or a car with a driver, is effectively mandatory here, not an upsell. Do not draft an AlUla itinerary that assumes the customer can get between sites unaided.
Budget - budget/mid tier, has a desk at AlUla International Airport (ULH) and is reported as the most-used company there, from roughly USD 42/day.
Lumi Car Rental - Saudi operator, desk at the airport.
Alrehaili Car Rental - local operator, desk at the airport.
Lamar Luxury Car Rental - premium tier, available in AlUla though not necessarily an airport desk.
Europcar - international chain, operates in AlUla.
No independent review scores or government licensing registry listings were found for the AlUla-specific operators during this research. The local operators here are much less documented online than the Riyadh and Jeddah chains, so treat all of them as unverified on reputation and lean toward the recognisable names, or toward a driver arranged through our own trusted-provider list, where reliability matters more than price.
Other options, all with real limitations worth knowing: airport taxis exist but in limited numbers on a first-come first-served basis, so they cannot be relied on for a timed transfer. Careem and Uber technically cover AlUla but the number of active vehicles is very small, so a customer should not plan around hailing a ride. There is also a local ride-share app, Darb AlUla, built specifically for getting to AlUla's sites, which is worth mentioning to independent-minded travellers.
International visitors driving themselves must carry both their home driving licence and an International Driving Permit. This catches people out and is worth stating plainly to any customer planning to self-drive.

Attractions:
Hegra (Mada'in Salih / al-Hijr) - open, Saudi Arabia's first UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Nabataean tomb complex, about 22 km north of AlUla town. Access is via official Experience AlUla tours rather than free wandering: a Hegra Day Tour, a Hegra Premium Tour, and a hop-on hop-off option all exist. Specific current ticket prices were not confirmed in this research, so the team should pull live pricing from experiencealula.com at quoting time rather than stating a figure. Booking ahead is strongly advisable, this is the region's headline site.
Maraya - operating, the mirrored concert hall, visitable via an official Maraya Tour. It also hosts events, so on event days general visiting may be restricted, confirm before scheduling.
Elephant Rock (Jabal AlFil) - a free, unticketed natural rock formation with no set opening hours, accessible in the open. Best at sunrise or sunset, both for heat and because that is when it photographs well. There is an informal seating and refreshment area on site. This is a genuine no-cost highlight, good for balancing a budget.
AlUla Old Town - restored heritage district, walkable, with shops and cafes. Free to walk; specific hours were not confirmed, and like everything here it is far more pleasant outside midday heat.
Sharaan Nature Reserve - operating, a protected reserve. Access is generally via organised experiences rather than independent entry, and capacity is limited, so it needs booking ahead. Current pricing and availability not confirmed in this research, check at quoting time.
General note: nearly all AlUla ticketing runs through the official Experience AlUla platform, which makes it a single reliable place for the team to verify hours, prices and availability. That is the right source to check before publishing any AlUla itinerary, rather than third-party listings which go stale.

Flights:
ULH (Prince Abdul Majeed bin Abdulaziz / AlUla International) serves the region, with domestic connections from Riyadh and Jeddah and some seasonal international service that expands during the cooler high season. Most international customers will connect through Riyadh (RUH) or Jeddah (JED). No flight times or prices researched, the team prices these separately.
