Scope note:
Uzungöl is a lake village in the mountains about 95 km south-east of Trabzon, roughly a 1.5 to 2 hour drive on a winding road. It is a short-stay destination: one or two nights, usually as part of a north-east Turkey loop with Ayder, Hamsiköy, Sümela and Trabzon itself. Plans for it should treat Trabzon as the arrival airport and the base for anything the village cannot supply.

Trip ideas, unverified:
The following came from a Saudi travel post circulating in September 2026 setting out a 4 to 5 day northern Turkey run. Names were written in Arabic and Latin script on the post and are transliterated here as given. Nothing below has been checked for opening hours, prices, current operation or even spelling. Treat every name as a candidate to verify, not as a recommendation, and never quote one to a customer as our own pick without confirming it first.
Day 1, Uzungöl - breakfast at Kom or at a place written as Imad Gholu. A loop around the lake plus the waterfall. Lunch at Sofra or Seflerin Yeri. Coffee at Pi Cafe Uzungol. Late afternoon at the Uzungol viewpoint and Demirkapi.
Day 2, Ayder - breakfast at the lodge or hut. Eskitoglu waterfall. Lunch at Haci Mustafa. Gelin Tulu waterfall. The Firtina river and the Ottoman arch bridges. Lunch option at a restaurant written as Osmanli. Zil Kale castle and Palovit waterfall. Ayder plateau.
Day 3, Hamsikoy and Trabzon - breakfast in Hamsikoy village, which is known for its rice pudding. Karaca cave. Trabzon Meydan square. Lunch at Serender. Ataturk Kosku mansion. Ayasofya mosque.
Day 4, Sera and Sumela - breakfast at Sera Golu Assor Cafe. Sera lake. A strawberry farm. Trabzon Botanik garden. Sumela monastery.

What a plan writer should check before using any of it:
Sumela monastery has been through long restoration closures and partial reopenings; confirm it is open and whether the upper sections are accessible.
Zil Kale, Karaca cave and the Ayder plateau road close or become hazardous in winter snow; the whole itinerary above reads as a summer one.
Ayder is about 3.5 hours from Uzungol by road, so day 1 and day 2 above are not a single base. A plan that moves between them needs a hotel change or a very long driving day, and should say so.

Getting there:
Trabzon airport (TZX) is the arrival point, with domestic connections through Istanbul and some direct Gulf seasonal service. From the airport a private transfer or a rental car is the practical way to Uzungol; there is no useful public transport for a visitor with luggage. The road climbs steadily and is slow in fog, which is common in the afternoon.

Season:
Summer is the busy season, and Gulf visitors are a large share of it, which is why the village has Arabic menus and signage. July and August are crowded and cost more. Late spring and early autumn are quieter and greener. Winter is cold, wet and snowy, with some of the higher plateau roads closed.
